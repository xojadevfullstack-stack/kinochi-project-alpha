# Hisobot C0.5: fix(push): throttle new episode notifications with retry and cleanup

**Commit**: `47c46ec`
**Sana**: 2026-10-27

## 1. Vazifa va bajarilgan ishlar
Serialning yangi qismi qo'shilganda obunachilarga Telegram orqali yuboriladigan xabarnomalar tizimi takomillashtirildi (`backend/app/api/v1/series_subscriptions.py`):
1. **Throttling**: Har bir yuborilgan xabar orasida `asyncio.sleep(0.05)` (maksimal 20 xabar/sekund) cheklovi o'rnatildi.
2. **Pauza**: Har 500 ta xabardan so'ng `asyncio.sleep(1.0)` pauza qo'shildi.
3. **429 Retry-After**: Telegram `429 Too Many Requests` javob qaytarsa, `retry_after` parametri bo'yicha kutib 1 marta qayta urinish yo'lga qo'yildi.
4. **403 Forbidden (Unsubscribe)**: Botni bloklagan foydalanuvchilar (`403 Forbidden`) aniqlanganda, ularning obunasi bazadan (`SeriesSubscriptionModel`) o'chirib tashlanadi.
5. **Non-blocking BackgroundTasks**: `/api/v1/series/{series_id}/notify-new-episode` endpointi xabarnomani fon vazifasi sifatida (`BackgroundTasks.add_task`) rejalashtirib, mijozga zudlik bilan `{"success": true, "notified_count": N, "total_subscribers": N}` javobini qaytaradi.
6. **Yangi testlar**: `backend/tests/test_notify_throttling_c05.py` da 4 ta test yozildi:
   - `test_notify_throttling_600_subscribers` (600 obunachi, 0.05s va har 500 tada 1.0s sleep tekshiruvi)
   - `test_notify_retry_after_429` (429 Retry-After holatida kutish va qayta jo'natish)
   - `test_notify_forbidden_403_deletes_subscription` (403 olgan foydalanuvchini obunadan o'chirish)
   - `test_notify_endpoint_returns_immediately` (endpoint fon vazifasini chaqirishi va zudlik bilan javob qaytarishi)
